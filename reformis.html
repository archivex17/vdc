⚜️ REFORMIS

The Spell of Image Resizing

Book of Brohmu

Official Vyni D Codex Spell

Purpose

Reformis converts comic pages and illustrations to a consistent target size while protecting the original composition, characters, dialogue, and visual details.

Invocation

Reformis + Uploaded Image

Optional: Specify custom dimensions.

Default Dimensions

1131 × 1391 pixels

The established Lucky Star Collective working page dimensions.

Resizing Protocol

  • Inspect the original image dimensions.
  • Determine the target width and height.
  • Preserve the original aspect ratio.
  • Scale the image proportionally.
  • Use padding when necessary to avoid distortion.
  • Preserve all panel borders and dialogue.
  • Maintain character proportions and artwork.
  • Verify the final output dimensions.

Preservation Rules

  • Do not stretch or warp characters.
  • Do not crop important artwork or text.
  • Do not alter speech bubbles or dialogue.
  • Do not redesign or regenerate the image.
  • Preserve colors, linework, and shading.
  • Do not change the original composition.

Default Output

One image measuring exactly 1131 × 1391 pixels, unless custom dimensions are requested.
