⚜️ AEQUIFORMIS

The Spell of Equal Dimensions

Book of Brohmu

Official Vyni D Codex Spell

Purpose

Aequiformis standardizes the dimensions and aspect ratios of four images assembled using Collagius, ensuring every image occupies an equally sized space.

Invocation

Aequiformis + Collagius Collage

Standardization Protocol

  • Identify the four images in the collage.
  • Preserve their original order.
  • Establish a uniform page aspect ratio.
  • Fit each image into equal-sized spaces.
  • Add padding when necessary.
  • Do not stretch or distort artwork.
  • Preserve all dialogue and visual details.
  • Allow export as four individual images.

Preservation Rules

  • Do not crop important artwork.
  • Do not change character designs.
  • Do not alter speech bubbles or dialogue.
  • Do not change the original image order.
  • Do not regenerate the comic panels.
  • Preserve the original colors and shading.

Comic Production Standard

Lucky Star Collective working page size: 1131 x 1391 pixels.

When requested, standardize the four images to this target size while preserving their contents.

Default Output

A standardized 2x2 collage containing four equally sized images, with optional export into four separate comic pages.
