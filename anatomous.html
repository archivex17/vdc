⚜️ ANATOMOUS

The Spell of Anatomical Analysis

Book of Brohmu

Official Vyni D Codex Spell

Purpose

Anatomous examines character anatomy, body proportions, posture, balance, and physical movement to identify anatomical inconsistencies in artwork.

The spell provides precise correction recommendations while respecting the established character design and artistic style.

Invocation

Anatomous + Uploaded Image

Optional: Specify a character, body part, or pose for analysis.

Anatomical Analysis Protocol

  • Identify the character being analyzed.
  • Examine head-to-body proportions.
  • Inspect shoulder, torso, and hip alignment.
  • Evaluate arm and leg proportions.
  • Check joint positioning and articulation.
  • Analyze hand and foot anatomy.
  • Evaluate balance and weight distribution.
  • Inspect posture and center of gravity.
  • Identify unnatural bending or stretching.
  • Check for missing or duplicated limbs.
  • Assess whether the pose supports its intended action.
  • Distinguish intentional stylization from errors.

Combat Pose Analysis

  • Evaluate stance stability.
  • Identify the character's supporting leg.
  • Examine hip and shoulder rotation.
  • Check the direction of physical force.
  • Analyze punch and kick mechanics.
  • Evaluate movement and momentum.
  • Identify poses that appear stiff or unnatural.
  • Recommend corrections for believable action.

Preservation Rules

  • Preserve the character's official design.
  • Respect intentional cartoon proportions.
  • Do not alter unrelated characters.
  • Do not modify backgrounds or dialogue.
  • Do not change the artistic style.
  • Do not automatically edit the image.
  • Provide correction recommendations first.
  • Only apply edits when requested.

Related Spells

Differentation: Compares character appearances against their official reference designs.

Fixus: Applies targeted corrections to the selected character.

Collim: Maintains visual consistency across comic artwork.

Default Output

A detailed anatomical analysis identifying proportion errors, balance problems, pose inconsistencies, and recommended corrections.

When appropriate, provide a precise editing prompt that can be used with Fixus.
