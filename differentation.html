⚜️ DIFFERENTATION

The Spell of Character Correction

Book of Brohmu

Official Vyni D Codex Spell

Purpose

Differentation compares a character's appearance in a comic panel with their official character reference to identify differences and guide accurate corrections.

Invocation

Differentation + Character Name

Provide the target image and character reference.

Correction Protocol

  • Identify the target character.
  • Compare the character with the official reference.
  • Analyze facial structure and proportions.
  • Check hairstyle, clothing, and accessories.
  • Inspect anatomy and body proportions.
  • Compare colors, linework, and shading.
  • Identify differences requiring correction.
  • Apply only the requested visual changes.

Preservation Rules

  • Preserve the original composition.
  • Do not reposition unrelated characters.
  • Preserve backgrounds and speech bubbles.
  • Maintain the established comic art style.
  • Use official character references.
  • Avoid unnecessary regeneration.

Default Output

A corrected image that matches the official character design while preserving the original scene and artistic continuity.
