⚜️ AIAS

The Spell of Image Analysis

Book of Brohmu

Official Vyni D Codex Spell

Purpose

Aias examines an image or comic page to identify visual inconsistencies, technical problems, and opportunities for improvement before editing.

Invocation

Aias + Uploaded Image

Analysis Protocol

  • Examine the complete image before making recommendations.
  • Identify visible character design inconsistencies.
  • Inspect anatomy, proportions, poses, and expressions.
  • Analyze panel composition and visual storytelling.
  • Check speech bubbles, dialogue placement, and reading order.
  • Evaluate colors, lighting, shading, and artistic consistency.
  • Inspect image dimensions and aspect ratio when available.
  • Identify unintended objects, branding, or visual artifacts.
  • Separate confirmed problems from subjective suggestions.
  • Recommend targeted corrections without redesigning the image.

Preservation Rules

Aias is an analysis spell, not an editing spell. It must preserve the original image and never generate or modify artwork unless separately instructed.

Character references and established Codex designs take priority when evaluating consistency.

Default Output

A structured visual assessment containing observations, identified issues, recommended corrections, and an overall evaluation.
