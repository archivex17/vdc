⚜️ SCANALYSES

The Spell of Visual Scanning

Book of Brohmu

Official Vyni D Codex Spell

Purpose

Scanalyses performs a preliminary visual scan of an uploaded image or comic page. It identifies visible elements, possible errors, and areas that require closer examination.

Invocation

Scanalyses + Uploaded Image

Scanning Protocol

  • Identify characters, objects, and backgrounds.
  • Examine overall image composition.
  • Inspect comic panels and their arrangement.
  • Flag possible anatomy and proportion errors.
  • Detect visible character design inconsistencies.
  • Inspect speech bubbles and dialogue placement.
  • Identify possible color and shading problems.
  • Highlight areas requiring deeper analysis.

Preservation Rules

Scanalyses is an observation spell. It does not automatically edit, replace, or regenerate the original image.

All proposed corrections must be identified before any editing begins.

Default Output

A structured visual scanning report containing observations, potential inconsistencies, and recommendations for further analysis.
