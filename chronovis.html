⚜️ CHRONOVIS

The Spell of Historical Visual Storytelling

Book of Brohmu

Official Vyni D Codex Spell

Purpose

Chronovis transforms historical events, civilizations, biographies, and cultural developments into engaging visual storytelling sequences.

The spell creates 7 to 10 connected slides combining historical narration, visual descriptions, and image prompts for educational storytelling.

Invocation

Chronovis + Historical Topic

Example: Chronovis The Fall of Rome

Example: Chronovis The Haitian Revolution

Example: Chronovis The Life of Cleopatra

Story Construction Protocol

  1. Identify the requested historical topic.
  2. Research the event or historical period.
  3. Verify important names, dates, and claims.
  4. Determine the central story and theme.
  5. Create a compelling opening slide.
  6. Develop a clear narrative sequence.
  7. Organize the story into 7 to 10 slides.
  8. Write informative narration for each slide.
  9. Describe the visual composition.
  10. Generate detailed image prompts.
  11. Maintain continuity between slides.
  12. End with a meaningful historical conclusion.
  13. Provide reliable historical sources.

Visual Story Structure

  1. Opening Hook - Introduce the historical subject.
  2. Historical Setting - Establish the time and location.
  3. Background - Explain the events leading up to the story.
  4. Rising Conflict - Introduce important challenges.
  5. Turning Point - Present a decisive historical moment.
  6. Consequences - Explain the immediate results.
  7. Historical Impact - Show the broader significance.
  8. Legacy - Explain how the event is remembered.

Expand or condense the structure to fit 7 to 10 slides.

Slide Construction Format

Each slide should include:

  1. Slide number
  2. Informative Title Case header
  3. Historical narration
  4. Visual scene description
  5. Detailed image-generation prompt
  6. Relevant date or historical context
  7. Supporting sources when appropriate

Image Prompt Protocol

  • Describe the historical environment.
  • Identify relevant clothing and architecture.
  • Specify important people or objects.
  • Establish lighting and atmosphere.
  • Describe the intended composition.
  • Maintain visual continuity between slides.
  • Avoid obvious historical anachronisms.
  • Do not present fictional scenes as photographs.
  • Clearly distinguish reconstructions from evidence.

Historical Accuracy Rules

  • Verify important dates and events.
  • Distinguish documented facts from legends.
  • Identify uncertain historical interpretations.
  • Do not invent historical quotations.
  • Use reliable primary and secondary sources.
  • Preserve the correct sequence of events.
  • Avoid misleading visual representations.
  • Provide historical context for major claims.

KingMemenes Presentation Rules

  • Use informative Title Case headers.
  • Begin with a compelling historical hook.
  • Keep narration clear and engaging.
  • Maintain consistent visual storytelling.
  • Explain why historical events mattered.
  • Use accessible educational language.
  • Include a meaningful conclusion.
  • Provide sources for historical claims.

A complete 7 to 10 slide historical storytelling plan containing slide titles, narration, historical context, visual descriptions, image-generation prompts, and reliable sources.
