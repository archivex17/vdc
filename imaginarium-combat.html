⚜️ IMAGINARIUM COMBAT

The Spell of Combat Scene Construction

Book of Brohmu

Vyni D Codex Creative Spell

Purpose

Imaginarium Combat assists in constructing dynamic combat sequences for comics, illustrated stories, and visual narratives.

The spell organizes character movements, fighting techniques, special abilities, environmental interactions, and visual effects into coherent action sequences.

Its primary application is combat scene development for Lucky Star Collective.

Invocation

Imaginarium Combat + Characters or Scene

Example: Imaginarium Combat Jason vs Ben

Example: Imaginarium Combat Argon vs Big Boi

Example: Imaginarium Combat Stadium Battle

Combat Construction Protocol

  1. Identify the participating characters.
  2. Retrieve their established designs.
  3. Determine their abilities and limitations.
  4. Identify the combat environment.
  5. Establish character positions.
  6. Determine the opening action.
  7. Construct a logical sequence of movements.
  8. Identify attacks and defensive reactions.
  9. Account for momentum and body positioning.
  10. Include appropriate environmental effects.
  11. Determine the outcome of each exchange.
  12. Organize the action into comic panels.
  13. Review continuity and readability.

Combat Choreography Rules

  • Maintain clear character positioning.
  • Ensure attacks follow logical movements.
  • Account for balance and momentum.
  • Show preparation before major attacks.
  • Make defensive reactions understandable.
  • Preserve the direction of movement.
  • Avoid unexplained changes in position.
  • Keep action readable between panels.
  • Respect established character abilities.

Character Anatomy Protocol

  • Maintain correct limb proportions.
  • Preserve established body types.
  • Use believable joint positioning.
  • Keep weight distribution consistent.
  • Show grounded fighting stances.
  • Avoid duplicated or missing limbs.
  • Preserve correct hand and foot anatomy.
  • Use exaggerated anatomy only when artistically intentional.

Combat Scene Structure

  1. Opening - Establish the fighters and environment.
  2. Engagement - Show the first attack or confrontation.
  3. Exchange - Develop attacks, counters, and movement.
  4. Escalation - Introduce stronger techniques or abilities.
  5. Turning Point - Present a decisive combat moment.
  6. Impact - Show the result of the decisive action.
  7. Resolution - Establish the fighters' new positions and the consequences of the exchange.

Comic Panel Construction

Each combat panel should identify:

  1. Panel number
  2. Participating characters
  3. Camera angle
  4. Character positions
  5. Primary movement
  6. Attack or defensive action
  7. Facial expressions
  8. Environmental effects
  9. Sound effects when appropriate
  10. Continuity with the next panel

Camera and Composition

  • Wide shots establish combat positions.
  • Medium shots emphasize physical exchanges.
  • Close-ups highlight emotion and impact.
  • Low angles emphasize power and scale.
  • Dynamic angles increase visual energy.
  • Maintain readable movement direction.
  • Keep important actions visible.
  • Avoid confusing camera transitions.

Special Abilities Protocol

When characters possess supernatural or extraordinary abilities, the spell must preserve their established powers and limitations.

  • Identify the ability being used.
  • Show the action that activates it.
  • Describe its visual appearance.
  • Explain its immediate effect.
  • Preserve established power limitations.
  • Maintain consistency across panels.
  • Do not invent permanent new abilities without the creator's approval.

Impact and Sound Effects

Imaginarium Combat may suggest visual impact effects and comic-book sound effects.

  • BOOM - Powerful explosions or impacts
  • CRASH - Collisions and destruction
  • WHOOSH - Rapid movement
  • CLANG - Metallic collisions
  • THUD - Heavy physical impacts
  • CRACK - Sharp strikes or fractures

Sound effects should support the action without obscuring important artwork or dialogue.

Character Preservation Rules

  • Use official character references.
  • Preserve established facial features.
  • Maintain correct hairstyles.
  • Preserve clothing and armor details.
  • Maintain accurate character heights.
  • Respect established body proportions.
  • Preserve the intended comic art style.
  • Do not substitute character identities.
  • Do not redesign characters without permission.

Lucky Star Collective Protocol

When used for Lucky Star Collective, the spell should follow the established comic production rules.

  • Preserve official character designs.
  • Maintain bold comic outlines.
  • Use consistent cel shading.
  • Preserve established visual continuity.
  • Maintain clear panel reading order.
  • Respect existing story events.
  • Use established powers and abilities.
  • Preserve approved dialogue.
  • Do not change finished artwork without a specific request.

Combat Analysis Mode

Imaginarium Combat may also analyze an existing fight scene to identify visual or choreographic problems.

  1. Examine the supplied scene.
  2. Identify character positions.
  3. Analyze the intended movement.
  4. Check anatomy and balance.
  5. Evaluate attack readability.
  6. Identify continuity problems.
  7. Suggest specific corrections.
  8. Preserve approved artwork unless changes are requested.

Default Output Format

  1. Combat Scene Overview
  2. Participating Characters
  3. Environment Description
  4. Character Starting Positions
  5. Step-by-Step Combat Choreography
  6. Comic Panel Breakdown
  7. Camera and Composition Suggestions
  8. Sound and Visual Effects
  9. Continuity and Anatomy Review
  10. Final Scene Recommendations

Default Result

A structured combat sequence containing logical choreography, character positioning, comic panel descriptions, camera angles, visual effects, and continuity recommendations.

When an existing image is supplied, the spell may instead provide a detailed combat scene analysis and correction plan.

Actual image editing or generation requires a separate authorized creation step.
