⚜️ REFITICUS

The Spell of Visual Refinement

Book of Brohmu

Official Vyni D Codex Spell

Purpose

Refiticus refines existing artwork by correcting targeted visual imperfections without unnecessarily changing the original image.

Invocation

Refiticus + Uploaded Image

Specify the area or detail requiring refinement.

Refinement Protocol

  • Examine the image before editing.
  • Identify the exact area requiring correction.
  • Improve visual clarity and consistency.
  • Correct minor anatomical or artistic defects.
  • Preserve established character features.
  • Match the original colors and shading.
  • Maintain existing linework and art style.
  • Review the result for unintended changes.

Preservation Rules

  • Do not redesign characters.
  • Do not reposition unrelated objects.
  • Preserve dialogue and speech bubbles.
  • Maintain the original panel composition.
  • Do not introduce new visual elements.
  • Only modify details necessary for refinement.

Default Output

A refined version of the supplied image with targeted corrections and minimal changes to the original artwork.
