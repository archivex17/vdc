⚜️ LEGATUMUS

The Spell of Historical Figures

Book of Brohmu

Official Vyni D Codex Spell

Purpose

Legatumus discovers and organizes historical figures according to a selected civilization, historical period, profession, achievement, or research theme.

The spell highlights influential, overlooked, controversial, and lesser-known individuals whose lives offer opportunities for historical education and storytelling.

Invocation

Legatumus + Historical Topic

Example: Legatumus Ancient Egypt

Example: Legatumus Forgotten Inventors

Example: Legatumus Women of Ancient Rome

Historical Discovery Protocol

  1. Identify the requested historical subject.
  2. Determine the relevant time period.
  3. Identify historical figures matching the topic.
  4. Include prominent and lesser-known people.
  5. Verify names and historical identities.
  6. Identify dates of birth and death when known.
  7. Explain each person's historical significance.
  8. Describe major accomplishments or actions.
  9. Distinguish documented facts from legends.
  10. Provide reliable research sources.
  11. Organize discoveries into a numbered list.
  12. Allow deeper research on selected figures.

KingMemenes Research Format

Each historical figure should include:

  1. Full name or historically recorded name
  2. Approximate lifetime
  3. Civilization or geographic region
  4. Historical role or profession
  5. Major achievements or actions
  6. Historical significance
  7. Interesting or lesser-known facts
  8. Reliable research sources
  9. Potential KingMemenes content angle

Verification Rules

  • Do not invent historical figures.
  • Verify important biographical claims.
  • Identify uncertain or disputed dates.
  • Distinguish mythology from documented history.
  • Avoid presenting legends as proven facts.
  • Use reliable historical sources.
  • Respect the requested time period.
