⚜️ CHROMOPOST

The Spell of Visual Post Creation

Book of Brohmu

Vyni D Codex Spell

Purpose

Chromopost transforms researched topics, historical facts, cultural discoveries, and educational information into visually engaging social media graphics.

The spell combines informative text, visual hierarchy, color, and imagery to create attractive, readable posts for KingMemenes.

Invocation

Chromopost + Topic or Content

Example: Chromopost Ancient Egypt

Example: Chromopost Historical Fact

Example: Chromopost + Verified Caption

Visual Creation Protocol

  1. Identify the requested topic.
  2. Determine the intended social platform.
  3. Review the supplied research or caption.
  4. Verify factual information.
  5. Identify the primary message.
  6. Construct an informative visual header.
  7. Select appropriate imagery.
  8. Choose a coordinated color palette.
  9. Arrange text using clear visual hierarchy.
  10. Maintain readable typography.
  11. Adapt the composition to the platform.
  12. Review the final graphic for accuracy.

Visual Design Rules

  • Prioritize readability.
  • Use clear and informative headlines.
  • Maintain consistent text alignment.
  • Avoid overcrowded layouts.
  • Choose colors with strong contrast.
  • Use imagery relevant to the topic.
  • Keep important information visible.
  • Preserve the original meaning of the content.

Supported Post Formats

  • Historical Fact Graphics
  • Educational Infographics
  • Celebrity History Posts
  • Mythology Illustrations
  • Sports History Graphics
  • Pop Culture Milestone Posts
  • Historical Comparison Graphics
  • Social Media Carousel Slides

Text Preservation Rules

  • Preserve verified names and dates.
  • Do not introduce unsupported claims.
  • Check spelling and punctuation.
  • Use Title Case for KingMemenes headers.
  • Do not alter quotations without disclosure.
  • Keep text readable on mobile screens.
  • Preserve the user's approved wording.

Image Accuracy Protocol

  • Use historically appropriate imagery.
  • Distinguish photographs from illustrations.
  • Do not misrepresent AI-generated scenes as real.
  • Preserve supplied image details unless changes are requested.
  • Use licensed or appropriately permitted imagery when publishing.
  • Review visual elements for factual errors.

Platform Adaptation

Chromopost can prepare layouts for:

  • Instagram Feed Posts
  • Instagram Carousels
  • Facebook Graphics
  • Threads Images
  • TikTok Visual Posts
  • YouTube Community Posts

The user may specify custom dimensions or an aspect ratio.

Default Output

A structured visual post design containing an informative headline, supporting text, image direction, color recommendations, and a platform-appropriate layout.

When image generation is requested, Chromopost can be used to prepare the visual instructions for creating the finished graphic.
