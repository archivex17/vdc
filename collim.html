⚜️ COLLIM

The Spell of Visual Consistency

Book of Brohmu

Official Vyni D Codex Spell

Purpose

Collim examines multiple images or comic panels together to identify visual inconsistencies between recurring characters, objects, and artistic elements.

Invocation

Collim + Uploaded Images

Consistency Protocol

  • Compare recurring characters across images.
  • Inspect facial features, hair, and anatomy.
  • Check clothing, accessories, and colors.
  • Identify differences in proportions.
  • Compare linework, shading, and art style.
  • Identify continuity errors between panels.
  • Recommend targeted corrections.

Preservation Rules

  • Preserve original character identities.
  • Use official reference designs when available.
  • Do not change unrelated visual elements.
  • Maintain the intended artistic style.
  • Do not automatically edit images during analysis.

Default Output

A visual consistency report identifying differences between images and recommending corrections for improved continuity.
