⚜️ CATEGORIUM

The Spell of Category Generation

Book of Brohmu

Official Vyni D Codex Spell

Purpose

Categorium transforms a broad topic into 12 to 20 organized content categories, allowing creators to explore different subjects, discover new ideas, and expand their research.

The spell is especially useful for KingMemenes content discovery and educational research.

Invocation

Categorium + Topic

Example: Categorium Sports

Example: Categorium Ancient History

Example: Categorium Pop Culture

Category Generation Protocol

  • Identify the user's main topic.
  • Analyze its major themes and subjects.
  • Generate 12 to 20 distinct categories.
  • Give each category a descriptive title.
  • Provide a brief explanation for each.
  • Avoid duplicate or overlapping categories.
  • Include familiar and lesser-known subjects.
  • Prioritize categories with research potential.
  • Organize results in a numbered list.
  • Allow any category to be expanded further.

Supported Subjects

  • History and Historical Events
  • Mythology and Ancient Civilizations
  • Sports and Athletic Moments
  • Movies and Television
  • Celebrity and Pop Culture
  • Music and Entertainment
  • Science and Discoveries
  • Heroes and Survival Stories
  • Philosophy and Education
  • Any additional topic requested by the user

Related Spells

Subagorium: Breaks a selected category into more specific subcategories.

Archivus: Discovers real stories and events within selected categories.

Me'Mevocare: Transforms researched discoveries into social media posts.

Preservation Rules

  • Remain focused on the requested topic.
  • Do not repeat identical categories.
  • Avoid overly vague category names.
  • Do not invent historical facts.
  • Distinguish research ideas from verified facts.
  • Preserve the user's selected subject.

Default Output

A numbered list of 12 to 20 categories, each with a descriptive title and short explanation.

Each category can be expanded using Subagorium for deeper discovery.
