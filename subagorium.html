⚜️ SUBAGORIUM

The Spell of Subcategories

Book of Brohmu

Official Vyni D Codex Spell

Purpose

Subagorium takes a broad category generated by Categorium and divides it into 12 smaller, more specific subcategories.

Each subcategory becomes a focused research direction for discovering historical events, viral moments, interesting stories, and educational content.

Invocation

Subagorium + Topic + Selected Category

Example: Subagorium Sports - Funniest Sports Moments

Example: Subagorium Pop Culture - Legendary Celebrity Moments

Subcategory Generation Protocol

  • Identify the original topic.
  • Identify the selected parent category.
  • Analyze the category's major themes.
  • Generate 12 specific subcategories.
  • Provide a descriptive title for each.
  • Explain the focus of each subcategory.
  • Avoid overlapping research directions.
  • Include both popular and obscure subjects.
  • Prioritize discoverable real-world examples.
  • Organize results into a numbered list.
  • Preserve the parent category's context.
  • Allow further research within any subcategory.

Discovery Workflow

Step 1: Categorium

Generate broad content categories.

Step 2: Subagorium

Break a selected category into 12 focused subcategories.

Step 3: Archivus

Research real events and stories within a chosen subcategory.

Step 4: Me'Mevocare

Turn verified discoveries into KingMemenes social media content.

Preservation Rules

  • Remain within the selected category.
  • Do not repeat identical subcategories.
  • Avoid overly broad or vague titles.
  • Do not invent historical events.
  • Distinguish ideas from verified stories.
  • Preserve the original topic hierarchy.
  • Do not replace the parent category.

Default Output

A numbered list of 12 distinct subcategories, each with a short explanation of its research focus.

The user can select any subcategory for deeper discovery using Archivus.
