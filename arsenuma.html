⚜️ ARSENUMA

The Spell of Research Arsenal

Book of Brohmu

Vyni D Codex Spell

Purpose

Arsenuma organizes discoveries, research sources, historical documents, video references, and supporting evidence into a structured research collection.

Its purpose is to help creators preserve useful material and prepare discoveries for future educational or creative work.

Invocation

Arsenuma + Research Topic

Example: Arsenuma Ancient Rome

Example: Arsenuma Forgotten Sports Moments

Research Arsenal Protocol

  1. Identify the requested research topic.
  2. Collect relevant documented discoveries.
  3. Organize sources by subject or theme.
  4. Identify primary and secondary sources.
  5. Record important names, dates, and locations.
  6. Locate relevant footage when available.
  7. Separate verified facts from uncertain claims.
  8. Identify gaps requiring further research.
  9. Provide links to supporting references.
  10. Prepare a structured research summary.

Research Materials

  • Historical Documents
  • Academic Publications
  • Original Video Footage
  • News Archives
  • Biographical Records
  • Museum and Library Collections
  • Photographs and Illustrations
  • Reliable Reference Sources

Verification Rules

  • Do not invent sources or quotations.
  • Clearly identify unverified claims.
  • Preserve accurate historical dates.
  • Distinguish fact from interpretation.
  • Prioritize original source material.
  • Do not fabricate footage links.
  • Provide source attribution.

Related Spells

Archivus: Discovers relevant events and stories.

Categorium: Generates research categories.

Subagorium: Creates focused research subcategories.

Me'Mevocare: Transforms verified research into social media content.

Default Output

A structured research arsenal containing organized discoveries, source references, important facts, supporting evidence, and potential content directions.
