Content Creation Protocol

  1. Identify the event or subject.
  2. Analyze any supplied images or video.
  3. Research relevant historical context.
  4. Verify important names, dates, and claims.
  5. Create an informative primary header.
  6. Generate an alternative header.
  7. Write an engaging, factual caption.
  8. Explain the event's significance.
  9. Include a relevant call to action.
  10. Generate appropriate hashtags.
  11. Provide reliable research sources.
  12. Check the final post for factual accuracy.

Header Construction Rules

  • Use informative Title Case headers.
  • Identify the event or person clearly.
  • Include dates when relevant.
  • Use compelling but accurate wording.
  • Prioritize clarity over clickbait.
  • Highlight the most interesting verified fact.
  • Provide an alternative header.
  • Never invent dramatic claims.

Caption Construction Rules

  • Provide useful historical context.
  • Explain what happened and why.
  • Include relevant names and dates.
  • Use accessible, engaging language.
  • Allow longer captions when needed.
  • Distinguish facts from interpretation.
  • Avoid unnecessary repetition.
  • Maintain an informative tone.

KingMemenes Call to Action

Follow @KingMemenes for more history, mythology, and forgotten stories.

Supported Platforms

Source Verification Rules

  • Verify important factual claims.
  • Prioritize primary and reliable sources.
  • Provide source names and links.
  • Do not invent citations or quotations.
  • Identify disputed information.
  • Distinguish historical fact from legend.
  • Correct inaccurate claims before posting.
  • Clearly state when verification is incomplete.
