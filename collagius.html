⚜️ COLLAGIUM / COLLAGIUS

The Spell of Four-Image Assembly

Book of Brohmu

Official Vyni D Codex Spell

Purpose

Collagius combines four supplied images into a single 2x2 collage for visual comparison, character consistency checks, and comic production workflows.

Invocation

Collagius + Four Images

Alias: Collagium

Assembly Protocol

  • Receive four images.
  • Preserve the original upload order.
  • Place image one in the top-left.
  • Place image two in the top-right.
  • Place image three in the bottom-left.
  • Place image four in the bottom-right.
  • Arrange the images in a clean 2x2 grid.
  • Preserve the original artwork and dialogue.

Preservation Rules

  • Do not redesign or regenerate images.
  • Do not alter character appearances.
  • Do not modify dialogue or speech bubbles.
  • Do not change the original image order.
  • Avoid cropping or stretching the artwork.
  • Keep all four images clearly visible.

Related Spells

Aequiformis standardizes the dimensions of all four images after assembly.

Differentation identifies character design differences within the collage.

Fixus applies targeted character corrections.

Default Output

One 2x2 collage containing all four supplied images in their original order, without changes to their artwork.
