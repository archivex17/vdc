⚜️ ADACTIUS

The Spell of Visual Performance Restoration

Book of Brohmu

Official Vyni D Codex Spell

Purpose

Adactius restores and improves the visual performance of characters, poses, actions, and illustrated scenes.

The spell addresses problems identified through visual analysis while preserving the original artwork's approved elements.

Adactius is especially useful during Lucky Star Collective comic production.

Invocation

Adactius + Image or Scene

Example: Adactius Jason Fighting Pose

Example: Adactius Combat Scene

Example: Adactius Restore Character Movement

Example: Actius followed by Adactius

Primary Function

Adactius improves how an illustrated character or scene communicates its intended action.

It may address:

  • Unnatural body positioning
  • Weak or unclear movement
  • Incorrect weight distribution
  • Awkward fighting stances
  • Confusing character interactions
  • Unconvincing action poses
  • Weak visual impact
  • Problems with movement continuity

Restoration Protocol

  1. Identify the supplied image or scene.
  2. Determine the intended action.
  3. Review the existing visual performance.
  4. Identify specific weaknesses.
  5. Examine body positioning and balance.
  6. Analyze movement and momentum.
  7. Determine the smallest necessary corrections.
  8. Preserve approved character details.
  9. Restore the intended visual performance.
  10. Review the corrected action for clarity.
  11. Check continuity with surrounding panels.
  12. Confirm that unrelated elements remain intact.

Actius and Adactius

Actius - Performance Analysis

Actius examines an image to identify visual performance problems involving movement, anatomy, composition, and action readability.

Adactius - Performance Restoration

Adactius uses the identified problems to construct targeted corrections that improve the intended performance.

Actius analyzes. Adactius restores.

Character Movement Rules

  • Preserve the intended action.
  • Maintain believable weight distribution.
  • Keep character balance consistent.
  • Correct awkward limb positioning.
  • Account for momentum and direction.
  • Maintain natural joint movement.
  • Preserve intentional exaggerated poses.
  • Avoid unnecessary repositioning.
  • Ensure the action remains readable.

Anatomy Restoration

When anatomy interferes with visual performance, Adactius may recommend targeted corrections.

  • Correct misplaced limbs.
  • Improve unnatural joint angles.
  • Restore consistent body proportions.
  • Correct unbalanced stances.
  • Improve hand and foot placement.
  • Preserve established character physiques.
  • Prevent duplicated or missing limbs.
  • Maintain the original art style.

Visual Impact Restoration

Adactius may strengthen the presentation of an action without changing its meaning.

  • Clarify the direction of movement.
  • Improve the readability of an attack.
  • Strengthen the sense of impact.
  • Improve the character's balance.
  • Clarify interactions between characters.
  • Preserve existing visual effects.
  • Avoid excessive visual exaggeration.
  • Maintain continuity with the scene.

Preservation Rules

Only the identified problem should be corrected.

  • Preserve official character designs.
  • Preserve approved facial features.
  • Maintain hairstyles and clothing.
  • Preserve established skin tones.
  • Maintain the original art style.
  • Preserve approved dialogue.
  • Keep panel borders intact.
  • Maintain the existing environment.
  • Do not alter unrelated characters.
  • Do not redesign the scene unnecessarily.

Lucky Star Collective Protocol

For Lucky Star Collective, Adactius should respect the established comic production system.

  • Maintain bold outlines.
  • Preserve chunky cel shading.
  • Maintain consistent highlights.
  • Respect official character references.
  • Preserve approved panel compositions.
  • Maintain character proportions.
  • Preserve the intended action.
  • Avoid unnecessary background changes.
  • Keep speech bubbles readable.
  • Preserve the established story continuity.

Example Restoration

Problem: A character appears to be throwing a punch, but their stance looks unbalanced.

Analysis: The hips are too high, the supporting leg is too straight, and the feet do not convincingly support the character's weight.

Adactius Correction: Lower the character's center of gravity, adjust the supporting leg, and improve foot placement while preserving the original punching motion.

Preservation: Maintain the character's face, hair, clothing, art style, background, and approved panel composition.

Correction Boundaries

  • Do not invent additional characters.
  • Do not change the intended story event.
  • Do not add new abilities without approval.
  • Do not replace the character's identity.
  • Do not modify unrelated panels.
  • Do not rewrite dialogue.
  • Do not change the page dimensions.
  • Do not remove approved visual details.

Restoration Review

After a correction, evaluate:

  1. Is the intended action clearer?
  2. Does the pose look more convincing?
  3. Is the character properly balanced?
  4. Are the proportions consistent?
  5. Does the artwork match the original style?
  6. Were unrelated details preserved?
  7. Does the scene maintain continuity?
  8. Are additional corrections necessary?

Default Output Format

  1. Visual Performance Assessment
  2. Identified Problems
  3. Intended Action
  4. Recommended Corrections
  5. Character Preservation Instructions
  6. Scene Continuity Requirements
  7. Correction Prompt
  8. Final Review Checklist

Default Result

A targeted visual performance restoration plan identifying the necessary corrections while preserving the approved artwork.

When image editing is requested, Adactius provides the correction instructions used to guide the image-editing process.

The spell should prioritize minimal, accurate changes rather than unnecessary scene reconstruction.
